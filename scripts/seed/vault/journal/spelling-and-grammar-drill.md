# Deliberate mistakes

Every paragraph below has something wrong with it on purpose. Turn spelling and
grammar on and check that each one is underlined — and that the two underlines
are told apart.

## Spelling

I recieved the pacakge on Wendsday and it was completly diferent from what I
had orderd. Definately going to complain, seperately, about the servce.

## Grammar, not spelling

Every word in this paragraph is spelt correctly but the grammar is not. Me and
him goes to the shop on Tuesday. She don't want none of it. There is three
reasons why this happen.

## Should be left alone

This paragraph is correct, and nothing in it should be underlined. It exists so
that a checker underlining everything is as visible as one underlining nothing.

## Markdown that is not a mistake

**Bold** and *italic* and `inlineCode` and a URL like https://example.com are
not spelling errors, and a checker that flags them is reading the markdown as
prose.
