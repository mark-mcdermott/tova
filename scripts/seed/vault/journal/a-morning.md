---
title: A morning
section: journal
tags: [journal]
---

Woke before the alarm again. The light through the blind was the grey that
means rain later, and I lay there listening to the house settle.

Made coffee badly — too fine a grind, so it ran slow and came out bitter. Drank
it anyway, standing at the window, watching next door's cat pick its way along
the fence with that particular offended dignity cats have in the wet.

Started reading the book Sam lent me and got four pages in before my mind
wandered off to the thing at work, which is the whole reason I said I'd read
more in the first place.
