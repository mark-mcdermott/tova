# Bread

| Ingredient | Weight |
| ---------- | -----: |
| Flour      |  500 g |
| Water      |  350 g |
| Salt       |   10 g |
| Starter    |  100 g |

1. Mix flour and water, rest an hour.
2. Add starter and salt.
3. Fold every half hour, four times.
4. Shape, prove overnight in the fridge.
5. Bake at 250°C with steam for 20 minutes, then 220°C for 20 more.

> Baker's note: the dough is wetter than you think it should be. Trust it.
