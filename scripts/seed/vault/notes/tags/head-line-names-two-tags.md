A block head may name more than one tag, and then the text under it belongs to
all of them. Deleting one takes text the other was heading — the preview is
supposed to say so before anything happens.

#ephemeral #keep

This paragraph is headed by both. Purging #ephemeral takes it, even though
#keep was heading it too. That is the case the confirmation needs to warn
about.

---

#keep #archive

This one is headed by two tags, neither of them the target. It survives.
