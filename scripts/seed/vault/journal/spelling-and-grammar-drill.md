# Deliberate mistakes

Two checkers run here and they are not the same thing. Spelling is macOS's own,
and it knows the words you have added yourself. Grammar is Harper, on this
machine, from a dictionary that is fetched rather than shipped.

Each heading below says what should happen under it. Where nothing should be
underlined, that is the answer — not a checker that failed to load.

## Spelling — all of these

I recieved the pacakge on Wendsday and it was completly diferent from what I
had orderd. Definately going to complain, seperately, about the servce.

## Grammar — these, and the reason is worth knowing

Harper reads words, not sentences. Everything it catches here is a pair of
words it can look up.

She don't want none of it.

Their going to be late for they're meeting.

The dog wagged it's tail.

She ate a apple.

He is taller then me.

I would of gone if I had known.

The medicine had a positive affect.

This is is a problem.

i went to london last week.

## Grammar — none of these, and that is not a bug

These need the sentence parsed: which word is the subject, whether the verb
agrees with it. Harper does not do that, so it says nothing. Measured, not
assumed — the full dictionary misses them too, so it is not the slim one's
fault.

Me and him goes to the shop on Tuesday.

There is three reasons why this happen.

The dogs barks at night.

Her and me went to the party.

Yesterday I go to the shop.

It was raining, we stayed inside.

## Nothing at all

This paragraph is correct, and nothing in it should be underlined. It is here
so that a checker underlining everything is as visible as one underlining
nothing.

Note that "spelt" is spelt correctly. Harper calls it a spelling error; Tova
drops Harper's spelling notes on purpose, because the system's checker is the
one that knows the words you have added.

## Markdown, which is not a mistake

**Bold** and _italic_ and `inlineCode` and a URL like https://example.com are
not errors, and a checker that flags them is reading the markdown as prose.
