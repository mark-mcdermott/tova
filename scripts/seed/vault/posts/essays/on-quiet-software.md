Most software is loud. It wants to tell you about its new features, ask how
you're finding it, and show you a badge with a number on it.

A writing app has one job, which is to get out of the way. Everything it does
that isn't that is a cost.

## The test

Open it, write a sentence, close it. Count how many things happened that you
did not ask for. That number should be zero.
